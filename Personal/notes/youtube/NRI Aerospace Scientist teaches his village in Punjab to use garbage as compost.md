Do you know over 300 villages in Punjab are litter-free now?  
  
Watch this video to discover how our sustainable development program is helping build a cleaner, healthier Punjab.  
  
In rural Punjab, unmanaged waste is a major risk to both public health and the environment. This success story proves that large-scale behavioral change is possible when vision, expertise, and community action come together with a shared purpose.  
  
Chuhar Chak in Moga district of Punjab is now a transformed village, thanks to the dedication of Dr. Surinder Sharma, Aerospace Scientist, and Navrup Singh Gill, the village Sarpanch. They didn’t let their vision fade. By embracing every suggestion and working closely with our team, they helped ensure the waste management unit was implemented effectively in every step of the way.
